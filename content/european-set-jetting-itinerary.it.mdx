If you want to build a holiday around the screen, Europe makes it easy: dozens of
famous locations sit within a short flight or drive of one another. Here are
three loops that each string several together, plus how to pace them.

## Loop 1: the Mediterranean (about a week)

Start in [Taormina](/destinations/taormina), Sicily, where the
[San Domenico Palace](/locations/san-domenico-palace) anchored a season of
resort drama and the [ancient theatre](/locations/teatro-antico-taormina) frames
Mount Etna. Fly on to mainland Spain for the Dornish courtyards of the
[Real Alcázar of Seville](/locations/alcazar-seville). If time allows, add a
Greek-island coda on [Skopelos](/destinations/skopelos), home to the
[clifftop chapel](/locations/agios-ioannis-skopelos) of a certain singalong
wedding.

## Loop 2: Britain and Ireland (about ten days)

Base in Scotland first. The Highlands give you the
[Glenfinnan Viaduct](/locations/glenfinnan-viaduct) — the Hogwarts Express bridge
— and the glens of [Outlander](/titles/outlander). Drop south through
[central Scotland](/destinations/central-scotland) for Castle Leoch and
Lallybroch, then into England for [Bath](/destinations/bath) and
[Greenwich](/destinations/greenwich), where the Regency facades of
[Bridgerton](/titles/bridgerton) wait. A short hop to
[Northern Ireland](/destinations/county-down) closes the loop with Winterfell.

## Loop 3: Iberia (about a week)

Western Spain is one big period set. The medieval old town of
[Cáceres](/locations/caceres-old-town) and the castle at
[Trujillo](/locations/trujillo-castle) became the capital of
[House of the Dragon](/titles/house-of-the-dragon). Swing through
[Madrid](/destinations/madrid) for the real-life targets of
[Money Heist](/titles/money-heist), then north to the Basque coast for
Dragonstone.

## Pacing it

> Two or three locations a day is plenty. Set-jetting is best when you leave room
> to actually enjoy the place, not just photograph the frame.

- Book ticketed sites (Alcázar, castles, the steam train) before you travel.
- Group spots by region and rent a car where the locations are rural.
- Shoulder seasons — May and September — give you the light without the queues.
